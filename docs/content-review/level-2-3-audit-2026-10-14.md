## Scope and method

Review every field of all three perspectives against the complete corresponding
section of the pinned September 2024 CMMC v2.13 assessment guide: requirement,
all determination/ODP statements, potential assessment methods, Discussion,
Further Discussion, examples and assessment considerations. Check meaning,
missing concepts, advice, optional examples versus mandates, evidence limits and
whether each audience receives a useful explanation. Prior selected-field mapping
review does not substitute for this full-field comparison.

The coordinating Codex agent implements corrections after source comparison.
Independent AI reviewers read bounded batches without repository edits or nested
reviewers. At most two review workers run concurrently. Each receives one full
pass and one final delta check. No names, credentials or human approvals are invented.

## Maintenance and limits

`src/data/editorial-audit.test.ts` binds each reviewed control to the current
perspective object and attributed statement rows, and checks complete field and
control inventories, citations and source hashes. Fingerprints use SHA-256 of
UTF-8 `JSON.stringify(parsedObject)` with original object insertion and array order;
formatting whitespace is excluded. This matches objective-mapping fingerprints,
but differs from the older Level 1 recursively sorted-key baseline.

A changed field or source statement requires a new semantic comparison and a
dated record before refreshing any fingerprint. Changes to a perspective also
invalidate its objective-mapping binding; recompare those relationships separately.
Hashes detect stale reviews; they cannot establish review quality.
