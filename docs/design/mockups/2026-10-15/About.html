A little clarity
goes a long way.

Cybersecurity requirements can be hard to read. Cipher & Chalk keeps the official sentence on the page and then says, in plain words, what it is asking for.

This independent, open-source project covers all 149 CMMC controls, pinned to CMMC v2.13, September 2024. Each one carries the official requirement, quoted word for word with its page citation, and three perspectives written to explain it.

Who makes this.

I’m Wayland. I work in IT and compliance, and CMMC Level 2 is part of my day job: the system security plan, the policies, the evidence, and the meetings where a technical person and an assessor use the same word to mean different things. I started Cipher & Chalk because the explanation I kept wishing existed didn’t.

CMMC in one paragraph.

The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification is how the Department of Defense checks that its contractors protect the information it shares with them. Level 1 covers 15 basic safeguards for Federal Contract Information and is self-assessed. Level 2 covers the 110 requirements of NIST SP 800-171 for Controlled Unclassified Information.
