Authorized Access Control [FCI Data]

DecodeExplainEvidencePracticeRelated

Limit information system access to authorized usersa d, processes acting on behalf of authorized usersb e, or devices (including other information systems)c f.

[a]authorized users are identified;[d]system access is limited to authorized users; [b]processes acting on behalf of authorized users are identified;[e]system access is limited to processes acting on behalf of authorized users; and [c]devices (and other systems) authorized to connect to the system are identified;[f]system access is limited to authorized devices (including other systems).
