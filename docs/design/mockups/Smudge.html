Meet Smudge.

The eraser that lives on the chalk tray. Wooden back, felt face, and two eyes somebody drew on him in chalk. He is the thing that wipes the board between controls, so when the board changes, that was him.

  • Sits on the right end of the tray, on every screen
  • Never speaks. Eyes follow your cursor. One gesture per thing you do
  • Click him: a sneeze of chalk dust. Leave him a minute: he dozes off
  • Off switch in the footer; a plain eraser in print and under reduced motion

IdleBlinks. Eyes drift toward your cursor. A tiny puff of dust now and then.

ErasesYou change control: he leaps up, sweeps across the board, and the old text goes to dust. That is the Erase feature, with a face.

Hands you a chalkPick a perspective and he nudges that chalk up out of the tray.

SneezesClick or tap him. A burst of dust, and he settles. Also on a correct practice answer.

DozesAfter a minute with nothing to do. Any mouse or key wakes him.

OffFooter switch, print, or reduced motion: a plain eraser, no face, no motion.
