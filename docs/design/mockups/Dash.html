Meet Dash.

The ring in the logo is the letter C in Morse: dash, dot, dash, dot. One dash climbed out. It is a piece of chalk, a Morse signal, and the thing it does when you change controls.

  • Lives in the tray, not in a corner
  • Never speaks. One gesture per interaction, then still
  • Off switch in the footer; hidden in print and under reduced motion

IdleSits in the tray. Blinks every few seconds, slowly. That is all.

Hands you a chalkWhen you pick a perspective, Dash lifts that chalk out of the tray and tips it toward you.

ErasesOn a control change, Dash sweeps once across the board, eyes closed, and the old text goes to dust.

PointsOpen a glossary popover and Dash nods toward it. One nod.

DotA friend. Appears only when you finish a practice activity, and leaves. Twice a year, maybe.

OffReduced motion, print, or the footer switch: Dash becomes a plain stub of chalk in the tray.

Drawn as one SVG path with the chalk filter. Eyes are holes: the board shows through. — · — ·
Dash is the white chalk. It was there all along.
