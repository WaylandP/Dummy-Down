# NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3 — source record (2026-10-15)

`src/data/cmmc-rev3-map.json` places each of the 110 Level 2 controls of the
pinned CMMC v2.13 snapshot beside its counterpart in NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3
and adds one sentence, written by the project, on what changed. The Rev 3 view
of a control shows the two texts side by side with that sentence.

## What was read and how

- The requirement text of every `03.xx.yy` block in chapter 3, from the
  heading line to the line before DISCUSSION, read from `pdftotext -layout`
  output page by page (running headers, footers, the issue date and page
  numbers dropped, physical line wraps joined, a line ending in a hyphen joined to the next without a space). Withdrawn requirements keep
  their one-line note ("Incorporated into 03.01.12.", "Addressed by
  03.13.08.", "Consistency with SP 800-53 [8].", "Recategorized as NCO.",
  "Technology-specific."). 130 blocks: 97 requirements and 33 withdrawn.
- Each Level 2 control maps to the Rev 3 number its own number names
  (`3.1.1` → `03.01.01`): all 110 resolve, 77 to a requirement and 33 to a
  withdrawn note. PDF and printed page numbers are recorded per block.
- The change sentence per control (`unchanged`, `changed`, `new-odp`,
  `withdrawn`) was written by reading the pinned Rev 2 sentence (the guide's
  quotation in `cmmc-213-catalog.json`) against the Rev 3 text. "New ODP"
  marks a Rev 3 text that introduces an organization-defined assignment or
  selection the Rev 2 sentence did not have; a withdrawn note repeats, in
  Rev 3's own words ("incorporated into", "addressed by"), where the
  requirement went. Counts: 8 unchanged, 31 changed,
  38 with a new ODP, 33 withdrawn.
- `src/data/cmmc-rev3.test.ts` locks the mapping, the digest, the shape of
  each note and the counts.

## Limits

- CMMC assesses against NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 (32 CFR § 170.14). DoD has
  said Revision 3 will come in through future rulemaking; the Rev 3 view says
  so once and is for reading ahead only.
- The change sentences are the project's reading, not NIST's or DoD's
  analysis, and have not been reviewed by a qualified assessor. The two
  quoted texts control wherever a sentence and a text differ.
- Rev 3's DoD organization-defined parameter values (the April 2025 DoD
  memorandum the CMMC FAQ cites) are not part of this record.
