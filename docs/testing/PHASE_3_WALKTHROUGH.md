# Phase 3: learn, practice and return

Start at [Cipher & Chalk](https://cipherandchalk.vercel.app). This walkthrough covers
implemented engineering, not qualified content approval or a compliance assessment.
Everything taught remains a visibly marked educational draft.

## A ten-step visit

1. On Home, search for **AC.L1-b.1.i** and open **Authorized Access**.
2. Read the source quotation and **Sources & publication context**. The catalog is
   the September 2024 CMMC v2.13 snapshot; it does not assert current applicability.
3. Compare **Beginner**, **IT & Cyber** and **Compliance**. Each teaches from a
   different perspective while keeping the same requirement and edition.
4. Open **Watch the idea · 40 seconds**. Play/pause or select any of the four stages.
   **Read the full transcript** includes the meaning of every diagram. Reduced
   motion uses manual still stages; nothing starts automatically.
5. Open **Authorization** in the contextual terms, read it, then close it or press
   Escape. You stay in the same lesson and focus returns to the term.
6. Choose **Start or return to questions**. Try the tempting but incorrect idea that
   successful access proves authorization, then **Check answer**. Read why it fails.
7. Use **Try again** or continue. Finishing all three questions offers **Review the reasoning**. Open it to see
   your latest checked answers and useful takeaways. No score
   claims that an organization complies.
8. If useful, open **Learning progress on this browser**, read what is stored and
   enable saving. Interrupt another attempt after checking an answer; reload and
   return to the questions. Unchecked choices are deliberately temporary.
9. Mark a topic **Revisit later**. When saving is on, the **Practice** page marks
   finished situations and **Hide finished** takes them off its list. **Delete
   progress and stop saving** in a lesson, or turning the Practice page's **Keep
   my progress on this browser** line off, clears the record and returns to
   unsaved practice.
10. Browse another situation or level. Try the MFA and response-team examples below,
    keeping source quotations distinct from the fictional teaching scenario.

## Review that still needs people and external access

Leave each item unchecked until an actual review occurs. Record the reviewer,
date, exact publication/content version, device/browser/assistive technology,
observed task result, issue and repair evidence. Do not invent reviewers or infer
approval from tests.

- [ ] Qualified reviewers compare the 13 activities, 39 questions, 3 visual scripts,
      20 terms and relevant perspectives with their attributed source. Start with
      the [activity packet](../content-review/phase-3-activities-2026-10-14.md),
      [animation packet](../content-review/phase-3-animations-2026-10-14.md) and
      [qualified review instructions](../content-review/QUALIFIED_REVIEW.md).
- [ ] Verify current official-host bytes and applicability. The pinned September
      2024 snapshot remains explicit; do not silently replace its edition.
- [ ] Beginners, IT/Cyber learners and compliance readers try the ten steps. Ask
      what changed their understanding, which mode helped, where they got stuck,
      and whether the feedback made sense. Record observations, not invented scores.
- [ ] Actual screen-reader users check navigation, tabs, terms, SVG text equivalents,
      player states, quiz feedback, resume, reset and filtered-row focus.
- [ ] Check native browser zoom at 200%/400%, Safari/iOS and the relevant devices.
      Chromium viewport reflow, emulated preferences and axe are separate evidence.
- [ ] Owner accepts the visual experience after reviewing the real website.

Two pre-existing SI.L3-3.14.3e specialized-asset objective-mapping gaps remain open.
Thirteen activities are a focused sample, not interactive teaching for all 149
controls. The roadmap therefore keeps human-dependent Phase 3 requirements open.
